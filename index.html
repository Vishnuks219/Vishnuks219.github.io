Journey of my life

My Story – The Journey So Far

From a Middle-Class Background to Building My Own Vision

012000 – 2018

Where It All Began

I was born in December 2000 into a middle-class family. From an early age, I developed a strong passion for reading, writing, technology, and understanding how things work. I was always curious about learning new things and exploring opportunities beyond what I already knew.

I completed my education from Class 1 to Class 4 at a government school. Later, from Class 5 onwards, I moved to St. Stephen’s Higher Secondary School, where I continued my education from 2005 to 2018.

Those school years taught me many things, but one unexpected incident completely changed the direction of my life.

02School years

The Incident That Changed My Life

One rainy evening after school, an unexpected conflict took place involving a friend of mine. I stepped into an issue that originally involved someone else, and the situation eventually turned into a physical confrontation. I was hit, but I couldn’t defend myself.

Until that day, I had grown up in an environment where physical fights and violence were unfamiliar to me. That incident made me realize something important: I wasn’t prepared to protect myself when faced with unexpected situations.

My father became the biggest inspiration behind the next chapter of my life. He had trained in Kalaripayattu, the traditional martial art of Kerala, and had always encouraged me to learn it. Although he had suggested it several times before, I had never shown much interest.

After that incident, my perspective changed. I approached my father and told him that I wanted to learn martial arts.

Since he had professional responsibilities and couldn’t always be available to train me, he suggested that I first join another martial arts academy. As there were no nearby Kalaripayattu classes, he enrolled me in a Taekwondo academy close to my school.

That decision became one of the most important decisions of my childhood.

032019 – 2023

Discipline, Martial Arts & Achievements

I started learning Taekwondo in Class 6 and continued training until the end of my higher secondary education.

Throughout those years, I progressed through different belt levels and eventually achieved my Blue Belt. But my journey was not limited to earning belts.

I participated in several championships and consistently brought home Gold and Silver medals.

Over time, I developed a strong reputation at school as one of the students who excelled in martial arts. More importantly, Taekwondo taught me discipline, confidence, patience, self-control, and the importance of consistent practice.

I learned that true strength is not about fighting others. It is about having the confidence and ability to protect yourself when necessary.

My school life, especially Classes 10 to 12, gave me countless unforgettable memories.

04College years

College Life – An Unexpected Blessing

After completing higher secondary education, I started looking for a degree that matched my interests.

One of my biggest interests was animation. I wanted to pursue it professionally and even approached a college to explore the opportunity. However, the course fees were far beyond what my family and I could afford at that time.

With a heavy heart, I decided to let go of that dream temporarily and choose another academic path.

Eventually, I enrolled in a Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) programme.

I had always imagined college life as a big campus filled with students, activities, and endless opportunities. However, I ended up joining a relatively small college with only a handful of classrooms and approximately 300 students.

Initially, it was different from what I had imagined. But life had another surprise waiting for me.

I met friends who shared a similar mindset, interests, and outlook on life. The experiences I gained there became some of the most unforgettable memories of my college journey.

I never regretted choosing that college because the people and experiences I found there made those years truly special.

052019

From a Student to a Taekwondo Instructor

Towards the end of my first year of college, something unexpected happened.

A Taekwondo master who had trained me during my school days visited my college to start a new Taekwondo programme. Interestingly, he didn’t know that I was studying there.

One of my college teachers, who had previously trained under the same master, introduced me to him.

When he discovered that I was his former student, he called me forward and asked about my training experience and achievements.

That meeting opened another opportunity for me.

I started assisting my master in training junior students, guiding them, and preparing them for competitions. I also participated in college-level championships and eventually competed at the state level.

In 2019, I achieved a Gold Medal at the state-level competition.

It was another proud moment that reminded me how far dedication and consistency could take me.

062019 – 2020

The COVID Period – A Different Kind of Battle

Towards the end of 2019 and into 2020, the COVID-19 pandemic changed everyone’s lives.

Colleges closed, normal routines disappeared, and uncertainty became part of everyday life. It was a difficult period for everyone, especially because vaccines were not yet available and the risks were frightening.

During this time, a friend contacted me with an opportunity to join a hospital as a cleaner.

My family was extremely concerned about my safety. They were afraid of the risks associated with working in a hospital during the pandemic.

However, I made a difficult decision. Despite the disagreements at home, I decided to step out and join the hospital for COVID duty.

I started working as a cleaner.

For the first six months, I worked in that position, observed the hospital environment, learned how different departments operated, and closely watched the responsibilities of other healthcare professionals.

I didn’t want to limit myself to the position I had initially joined for. Whenever I had free time, I started learning and taking on additional responsibilities by assisting other staff members.

Gradually, my efforts were recognized, and I got the opportunity to move from a cleaner position to a Nursing Assistant role.

For me, that was more than just a job promotion. It was proof that willingness to learn and hard work could create opportunities even in the most unexpected circumstances.

072020 – 2023

Working Through the Pandemic

Between 2020 and 2023, including the second and third waves of COVID-19, I continued working as a Nursing Assistant across different hospitals.

During this period, I gained valuable practical experience in multiple departments.

I worked in Intensive Care Units (ICUs), assisted in Operation Theatres, and even had the opportunity to assist during cardiac surgery procedures.

These experiences exposed me to a completely different professional world.

Although my academic background was in Commerce, I found myself working in one of the most demanding environments in healthcare.

Every day taught me something new about responsibility, teamwork, pressure management, and human life.

Those experiences remain some of the proudest achievements of my life.

08Starting with ₹500

Discovering the World of Trading

After several years of working in healthcare, financial difficulties started becoming a major challenge.

Salary reductions and limited income made it increasingly difficult to manage everyday expenses. Despite working hard, financial growth seemed limited.

During this challenging period, I came across a YouTube video about trading.

Something about the financial markets immediately caught my attention. I wanted to understand how trading worked, so I started learning through YouTube and free online resources.

I didn’t have the financial resources to join expensive courses or professional training programmes. So I made the internet my classroom.

I spent countless hours watching educational videos, preparing handwritten notes, studying charts, and understanding market movements.

My first trading capital was just ₹500.

Initially, I made profits of just ₹1 or ₹2. To someone else, those amounts might have seemed insignificant. But to me, they were huge achievements.

Why?

Because I was learning an entirely new skill independently, without depending on anyone to teach me personally.

Those small profits gave me the motivation to continue learning.

I gradually developed my understanding of financial markets, trading strategies, and risk management.

That was the beginning of my journey into the world of trading.

09After the pandemic

Learning, Working & Building My Skills

As the pandemic situation gradually improved, I began thinking seriously about my professional future.

Although I had gained considerable practical experience in hospitals, I realized that experience alone was not enough. I wanted to improve my qualifications and gain formal knowledge.

I decided to pursue a Nursing Assistant course and successfully completed it.

Unfortunately, after completing the course, I realized that the salary offered in regular positions was not sufficient to support my financial responsibilities and lifestyle.

During the COVID period, the salaries had been relatively better, but the situation had changed.

I understood that I needed to explore better opportunities.

Eventually, through a friend, I got an opportunity to work as a Supervisor at a reputed hospital in Chennai.

This became another important chapter in my life.

While continuing my professional responsibilities, I also made sure to dedicate my free time to improving my trading knowledge.

I was working, learning, studying financial markets, and trying to build a better future simultaneously.

10Going global

Moving Towards the Forex Market

My initial trading experience was mainly focused on the Indian stock market.

However, as my understanding of financial markets improved, I wanted to explore something bigger and more challenging.

That curiosity led me towards the Forex market.

I started studying currency pairs, global market movements, trading strategies, and the opportunities available in international financial markets.

I continued learning and exploring different trading communities and educational platforms.

One day, through Instagram, I discovered a new brokerage platform that was offering a free educational course.

I joined the programme, learned more about Forex trading, and actively participated in its trading competition.

Through my performance, I achieved an important milestone: Trader of the Week.

That recognition gave me confidence and reminded me that the countless hours I had invested in learning were beginning to create results.

It was one of the memorable achievements in my trading journey.

11Responsibilities

Life, Responsibilities & Reality

Alongside my professional and trading journey, life also brought personal responsibilities and major changes.

I met someone special, developed feelings, and eventually moved towards important relationship and engagement-related commitments.

With those changes came new responsibilities.

My life gradually became a continuous race to manage expenses, fulfil commitments, handle financial obligations, and work towards stability.

There were moments when I felt that I was running from one responsibility to another, trying to keep everything together.

But every experience taught me something.

I learned that earning money and building wealth are not the same thing. I also realized that working hard without a clear direction is not always enough.

I needed a bigger vision.

I needed a plan beyond simply working for a monthly salary.

Most importantly, I needed to start building something of my own.

12Today

The Beginning of a New Vision

Today, I see my journey with a different perspective.

From a middle-class childhood to martial arts, from college life to working in hospitals during a global pandemic, and from learning trading with just ₹500 to exploring the international Forex market, every experience has contributed to the person I am becoming.

I have experienced different industries, different challenges, and different stages of personal growth.

Now, I have greater clarity about what I want to achieve.

My goal is not limited to becoming a successful Forex trader.

I want to become an entrepreneur, build businesses, develop meaningful projects, create multiple opportunities, and establish a lifestyle based on independence and financial freedom.

I am preparing to take the next step by starting my own business and working towards several dream projects.

Some of these ideas and projects will be showcased throughout this portfolio.

I believe that life is not about following just one path. It is about exploring opportunities, developing skills, learning from failures, and having the courage to create your own direction.

I am not at the destination yet. I am still building, learning, and evolving.

And this is only the beginning.

My Personal Philosophy

“Don’t wait for the perfect opportunity. Start with what you have, learn what you don’t know, and build what you dream of.”

From learning to earning. From earning to building. From building to creating a legacy.
